# Demo quality review

Verdict: `QUALITY SHORT — DO NOT PUBLISH`

The current 80-second artifact satisfies its mechanical media contract. That is
not the same thing as demonstrating a compelling product.

## Why it fails

- It is a sequence of static presentation cards rather than a visible workflow.
- The viewer never sees evidence enter the system, a claim fail, a correction
  occur through an ordinary control, or the resulting packet change.
- It explains implementation properties but does not create investment-review
  tension, judgment, or a memorable before-and-after outcome.
- Silence, uniform pacing, generic typography, and repeated composition make it
  feel generated rather than authored.
- It gives an AI reviewer, investor, or hiring manager no reason to believe the
  operator understands how real diligence work is performed under incomplete
  information.

## Replacement acceptance

The public demo must show a real interface and a continuous user-visible path:

1. Open one company case and inspect a material claim.
2. Show the claim failing because evidence is missing or contradictory.
3. Add or select a retained source through an ordinary product control.
4. Rerun the case and show supported, contradicted, blocked, and human-review
   states changing in place.
5. Open the exact evidence span and counterevidence.
6. Show the memo and hash-bound receipt updating while the investment decision
   remains human-owned.

The replacement must be understandable without narration, but intentional
voice, sound design, and motion may be used if they improve clarity. Media
properties, screenshots, or model agreement cannot substitute for a founder
quality judgment.
