# Revised evidence-to-decision direction

Effective date: 2026-08-31

This direction supersedes the breadth-first expert-readiness plan for the remainder of the current run. Commit `087739fa0c8751ca247bdbbca374d33090ac032c` preserves the pre-pivot work. The product objective is now the strongest feasible public vertical slice of an investor workflow, not a shallow implementation of a long-term platform roadmap.

## Product thesis

The Underwriting Intelligence Lab is a decision workbench for testing an investment case. It keeps evidence, human judgment, assumptions, empirical tests, deterministic transaction mechanics, and the resulting recommendation in one traceable deal state. The public demonstration uses fictional synthetic data and grants no investment authority.

The flagship path is AtlasGrid Systems:

1. Enter a realistic synthetic deal workspace.
2. Understand the current recommendation and the reasons it is blocked.
3. Search the deal package and distinguish what each item represents.
4. Record a private analyst observation.
5. review and explicitly approve or reject material assumptions without silently changing the model.
6. Inspect one meaningful empirical test with plain-language interpretation and limitations.
7. Change a precomputed transaction assumption and see deterministic return consequences.
8. Trace a decisive number to its source and calculation.
9. Review unresolved diligence and export a polished IC snapshot or underwriting packet.

Helios remains a companion proof that the contracts can support venture financing mechanics. It must not dilute the AtlasGrid first read.

## Component disposition

### Preserve and foreground

- The AtlasGrid and Helios synthetic source rooms and content-addressed manifests.
- The canonical decision ledger, binding hurdle logic, blockers, falsifiers, and explicit recommendation.
- Deterministic PE and VC transaction engines, scenario books, and downside cases.
- Registered investment metrics, formulas, source locators, and number-lineage drawer.
- Source search and analysis navigation.
- The randomized renewal-pricing test and its predeclared estimand, interval, diagnostics, and limitations.
- The one-page IC snapshot, underwriting packet, and separate technical appendix.

### Preserve but simplify or relocate

- Thesis graphs and value-creation plans move under supporting evidence.
- Methodology exposes one decision-relevant empirical test first; the full analysis register remains secondary.
- Audit hashes, receipts, provider details, regression ledgers, and implementation diagnostics remain available only through progressive disclosure or the technical appendix.
- Helios remains selectable but is not presented as equal first-read priority.

### Remove from the investor-facing story

- Multi-model orchestration as a product value proposition.
- Provider branding, agent theater, and claims that a model made or approved an investment decision.
- Enterprise-security, confidential-data readiness, production-firm adoption, proprietary-integration, and real-world investment-accuracy claims.
- Old generic demo artifacts, obsolete monolithic memo outputs, and duplicate controls that do not change a decision.

### Defer honestly

- Shared notes, permissions, SSO, durable multi-user audit history, confidential-document ingestion, and external data-room connectors.
- Real model/API connectivity unless one bounded integration can be implemented without weakening the flagship path.
- Consequential MCP writes. Any future assumption change or write operation must be staged and explicitly approved.

## Vertical-slice acceptance

The run may call the candidate deployable only if:

- AtlasGrid supports the full path above without a dead or deceptive control.
- Every investor-facing quantitative conclusion comes from the deterministic case state.
- Source classes are visually distinct and synthetic status is unavoidable.
- Notes are clearly labeled private-to-this-browser; no shared or secure-storage claim is made.
- Assumption decisions use a two-step review and confirmation flow and never silently rewrite engine output.
- The empirical test explains what was estimated, what it means, and what it cannot establish.
- Source search, lineage inspection, scenario controls, diligence navigation, and IC export work by keyboard.
- Technical diagnostics do not dominate any primary investment view.
- The static production build, public scanner, contract tests, and browser flows pass.
- A public URL is verified after deployment, or the precise deployment blocker is reported.

## Claim boundary

This is a public, synthetic portfolio demonstration of a disciplined evidence-to-decision architecture. It is not investment advice, a live data room, an enterprise-security claim, a production deployment at an investment firm, proof of real-world investment accuracy, or autonomous investment judgment.
