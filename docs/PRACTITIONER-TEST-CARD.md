# Underwriting Desk — 20-minute unguided test

Open the public Underwriting Desk without a walkthrough. Every company and record is fictional and synthetic. Do not upload confidential information.

## Your task

1. In your own words, explain what this product does, the current posture of one deal, and the risk that matters most.
2. Open **New deal** and import [`manifest.json`](https://underwriting-desk-delta.vercel.app/sample-package/manifest.json), [`deal.json`](https://underwriting-desk-delta.vercel.app/sample-package/deal.json), [`monthly_financials.csv`](https://underwriting-desk-delta.vercel.app/sample-package/monthly_financials.csv), and [`customer_arr.csv`](https://underwriting-desk-delta.vercel.app/sample-package/customer_arr.csv). Confirm what was recognized and whether anything is missing.
3. Explain why the 83.6% 11-month cohort-retention proxy matters against the separate annual-NRR screen even though the illustrative return screen clears.
4. Change one scenario input. Identify the financial and decision consequence, then confirm the canonical case was not overwritten.
5. Trace one important number to the source excerpt, row, period, or field that supports it.
6. Add a named investment observation.
7. Create a diligence issue, assign an owner and due date, update its priority and status, and record a resolution using your own name or initials.
8. Review one assumption and approve or reject it with a rationale.
9. Select evidence for a model challenge. Review the resulting proposal and accept, reject, or edit it as a named human.
10. State the resulting analytical posture, edit and export the IC working memo, and verify its scenario and unresolved conditions. Export the portable deal state so the work can be restored. Do not treat the provisional posture as a final IC authorization.

Your moderator will replace task 3 with one role-specific exercise so the session remains within 20 minutes.

## Final questions

- Why is this different from completing the same assignment in Claude or ChatGPT alone?
- What would stop you from trusting or using it on a real deal?
- What felt confusing, decorative, or incomplete?
- What do you believe Cooper personally contributed?
- Would this project improve Cooper's candidacy for an investing, finance, strategy, or applied-AI role? Why?
- Would you use this in an actual underwriting workflow? Under what conditions?

Please record task completion, time, confusion, errors, trust objections, and any help required in the [feedback template](practitioner-feedback-template.csv). The product has not passed practitioner validation until real participants complete this protocol.
