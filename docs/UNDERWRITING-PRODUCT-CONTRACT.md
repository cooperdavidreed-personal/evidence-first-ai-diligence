## Product thesis

Underwriting Intelligence Lab is a local, reproducible investment-workbench
reference implementation. It connects retained deal information to normalized
economics, explicit assumptions, econometric studies, downside cases,
illustrative human decision records, falsifiers, diligence requests, and
value-creation initiatives. Evidence controls are infrastructure, not the
headline.

The project is not an investment adviser, autonomous investment committee,
real-data accuracy benchmark, or production diligence system. AtlasGrid and
Helios are fictional. CoreWeave remains a separate, incomplete public-data
follow-on case.

## Fixed information hierarchy

The workbench uses five views in this order:

1. **IC Snapshot** — decision question, thesis and counterthesis, illustrative
   human decision, base/downside economics, decisive drivers, and falsifiers.
2. **Thesis and Evidence** — an acyclic thesis graph linking claims,
   counterevidence, assumptions, estimates, open questions, and kill criteria.
3. **Econometric Lab** — question, estimand, population, method,
   classification, uncertainty, assumptions, diagnostics, and limitations.
4. **Underwriting Room** — fully enumerated scenarios, sensitivities, debt or
   dilution mechanics, and return distributions.
5. **Value Creation** — evidence-linked initiatives, KPI baselines and
   targets, owners, milestones, value bridge, risks, and dependencies.

Evidence and provenance open from every view through a lineage drawer. They do
not displace the investment conclusion from the first viewport.

## Authority and decision records

The machine may recompute data, enforce contracts, propagate stale inputs,
classify analytical methods, and grade decision-record completeness. It may not
originate an investment recommendation.

The synthetic cases include illustrative, digest-bound human decision records:

- AtlasGrid: `REPRICE`, attributed to `Cooper David Reed — illustrative IC`.
- Helios: `INVEST`, attributed to `Cooper David Reed — illustrative IC`, with
  milestone-based conditions and open-condition counts.

The runtime packet retains `PENDING_HUMAN` for investment adjudication. A
decision record is a separate artifact whose status is
`DECISION_RECORD_WELL_FORMED` or `DECISION_RECORD_INCOMPLETE`. Any screenshot
containing a synthetic decision must show `SYNTHETIC — NOT INVESTMENT ADVICE`
and the illustrative-human attribution.

## Evidence-to-economics-to-action lineage

The shared graph contains three layers:

1. Evidence: retained artifact, locator, claim, counterevidence, and source
   control state.
2. Economics: normalized metrics, declared assumptions, estimates, scenarios,
   and returns nodes.
3. Action: decision conditions, diligence requests, falsifiers, and operating
   initiatives.

Every displayed number must reference a verified data field, another receipt,
or a visibly declared scenario assumption. Orphan constants, cycles, missing
units, missing periods, or stale upstream inputs force `HOLD`. A changed input
digest propagates `STALE` to every downstream node and decision record.

## Analytical classifications

Every study carries exactly one machine-enforced class:

- `ACCOUNTING_IDENTITY`: exact reconciliation or deterministic calculation.
- `DESCRIPTIVE`: summary of the frozen synthetic dataset without forward or
  causal inference.
- `PREDICTIVE_ASSOCIATION`: association only; uncertainty and denominators are
  mandatory and causal language is prohibited.
- `CAUSAL_SYNTHETIC_ONLY`: permitted only for a committed synthetic generator,
  assignment mechanism, seed, truth digest, estimand, and diagnostics.
- `SCENARIO`: conditional output from fully enumerated assumptions; not a
  forecast claim.
- `NOT_IDENTIFIED`: the design cannot support the requested inference.
- `HUMAN_JUDGMENT`: signed, dated, attributed, and never machine-generated.

## Case decision contracts

### AtlasGrid Systems

The asking-price case must miss at least one declared five-year hurdle. The
reprice or earnout case must clear 22% gross IRR and 2.0x MOIC while maintaining
the declared downside floor. A valid `REPRICE` record contains asking and
underwritten price/metric pairs, complete lineage, a per-driver value bridge,
the evidence that would have justified the marketed price, and signed handling
of any remaining material exception.

### Helios Compute Control

A valid conditional `INVEST` record contains entry price, ownership and
dilution math, runway, financing structure, milestone definitions, verification
sources, failure consequences, falsifiers, and open-condition counts. It cannot
be displayed as unconditional while any evidence-verification condition is
open.

## Visual contract

The reference class is an institutional investment memo combined with an audit
workpaper. The surface uses a warm light canvas, graphite typography, one
restrained cobalt accent, tabular numerals, compact spacing, visible units and
periods, point-of-view captions, and a separate treatment for synthetic cases.

Automatic rejection conditions include:

- chat inputs or assistant personas on a case surface;
- gradients, glow, AI art, generic bento cards, giant hero typography, or
  traffic-light decoration without defined semantics;
- confidence scores without a denominator and method;
- whole-document citations where a field or span exists;
- a number without lineage, period, unit, and analytical class;
- a synthetic screenshot without the fictional disclosure;
- an investment label without named illustrative-human attribution;
- chart decoration that does not change an underwriting conclusion;
- inaccessible scenario or lineage controls;
- hidden downside assumptions or probability weights;
- JSON, hashes, or model branding used as the primary hero content;
- a public model-performance, real-company accuracy, enterprise-readiness, or
  autonomous-decision claim unsupported by the mission evidence.

## Fail-closed acceptance

- Strict schemas reject additional fields and machine signers.
- Decision records with unresolved required fields remain incomplete.
- Every headline figure exposes a lineage path in no more than two interactions.
- Cycles, stale inputs, orphan constants, and missing evidence links block the
  affected decision record.
- Predictive studies without uncertainty fail validation.
- Causal labels without a committed synthetic assignment mechanism fail
  validation.
- All five views exist for both cases with no placeholder content.
- Existing evidence-kernel behavior and human-judgment boundaries remain
  unchanged.
- Model benchmark work remains `NOT AUTHORIZED` and `NOT RUN`.
