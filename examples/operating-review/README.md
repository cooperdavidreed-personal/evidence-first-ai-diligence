# Synthetic monthly source-change review

`case.json` is a reproducible fictional company workspace containing the exact original XLSX bytes, their SHA-256 digests, explicit mappings and three dated deliveries. No third-party company documents or private data are included. The UI opens a fresh copy from New deal → Try monthly operating review. Original workbooks can be downloaded from Evidence.

| July, Base scenario, USD ones | Original actual | Revised actual | Forecast |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Revenue (monthly) | 1,000,000 | 900,000 | 1,100,000 |
| Reported EBITDA (monthly) | 200,000 | 90,000 | 250,000 |
| Unrestricted cash (period end) | 800,000 | 350,000 | 900,000 |
| Funded debt (period end) | 3,000,000 | 3,300,000 | 3,000,000 |

The revised delivery is available August 10; the review cutoff is August 15. A subsequent August 20 delivery is deliberately outside that cutoff. The financial implications are revised net debt of $2.95m, $850k above forecast; 10% monthly reported EBITDA margin; and cash $150k below the analyst's $500k minimum. The minimum is an assumption, not a loan covenant. No annualized leverage, runway, transaction return or credit rating is inferred.

The analyst should revise the prior cash conclusion, assign a question about the funding plan and update committee materials. Neither the synthetic data nor these expected answers constitute independent practitioner validation.
