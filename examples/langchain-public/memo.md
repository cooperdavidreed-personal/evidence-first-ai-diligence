# LangChain / LangSmith public-only pre-diligence memo

As of: 2026-08-28. Status: `NOT EXECUTED — URL-ONLY SOURCES`.

## Working thesis

LangChain occupies a strategically relevant agent-engineering layer spanning
open-source orchestration and a commercial observability, evaluation, and
deployment platform. Its company-reported developer reach and trace growth make
the business worthy of primary diligence, but they do not establish revenue
quality or an investable entry price.

## Counterthesis

Open-source usage may convert weakly into paid LangSmith adoption. Hyperscalers
and model vendors can bundle adjacent capabilities. Open competitors can reduce
switching costs. Self-hosting requirements may create support burden, and usage
pricing may complicate buyer forecasting. Each proposition requires current,
independent evidence rather than narrative confidence.

## Disposition

`PROCEED_TO_PRIMARY_DILIGENCE_PENDING_HUMAN_APPROVAL`

This is not `INVEST`, a completed diligence conclusion, or investment advice.

## Required next evidence

- ARR and recognized revenue by product
- Gross margin after model, cloud, and support costs
- NRR, GRR, logo retention, ACV, and sales-cycle distribution
- Open-source-to-paid and free-to-enterprise conversion
- Customer concentration and independently selected reference calls
- Trace-volume definition and relationship to revenue
- Cloud, hybrid, and self-hosted mix and support burden
- CAC, payback, burn, and runway
- Cap table, valuation basis, preferences, and round availability
- Current security audit reports, scope, and exceptions
- Competitive win/loss evidence and workload portability
